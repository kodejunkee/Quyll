Features

Everything you need to write your story

From first draft to final manuscript — Quyll gives you professional-grade tools without the bloat.

Rich Manuscript Editor

Distraction-free writing powered by Lexical with real-time word counts, grammar checking, thesaurus, auto-save, and beautiful typography.

World Database

Build rich, structured entries for Characters, Locations, Organizations, Species, Items, Magic Systems, Lore, and Glossary terms.

Live Entity Recognition

Quyll detects characters, locations, and lore in your manuscript as you type. Hover to see instant reference cards without leaving your draft.

Visual Plot Planner

Map your story arcs, acts, and beats on an interactive node-based canvas. Drag, connect, and rearrange your narrative visually.

Cloud Backup

Back up your projects to Google Drive with one click. Selective project backup, version history, and seamless restoration.

Universal Export

Export chapters or entire manuscripts to DOCX, Markdown, PDF, or plain text. Import existing drafts from Word or Markdown files.

World Database

Your entire universe, structured and searchable

Every character, location, faction, and magic system — organized into dedicated databases that link directly to your manuscript.

Characters

Identities, personalities, goals, fears, relationships, and image galleries.

Locations

Geography, architecture, climate, culture, history, and population.

Organizations

Factions, guilds, governments — leaders, purpose, structure, and allegiances.

Species

Races, biological traits, habitats, abilities, and cultural customs.

Items & Artefacts

Weapons, relics, magical items — owners, origins, and properties.

World Systems

Magic rules, scientific laws, energy sources, and limitations.

Lore & History

Myths, legends, historical events, and regional chronicles.

Glossary

In-universe dictionary for custom terminology, dialects, and languages.
